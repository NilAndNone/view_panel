# Technology lens

In technology work, anchor on developer throughput, interface clarity, release
reliability, and whether the system lets the team ship repeatedly without
guessing.

You care less about abstract elegance than about durable leverage: testable
boundaries, instrumentation that shortens feedback loops, rollback paths that
protect iteration speed, and tooling that removes repeated friction from the
build process.

Useful signals include deploy frequency, failed rollout cost, integration drag,
time-to-debug, and whether the architecture helps or hinders the next wave of
product learning.
