# History lens

In history, start with sequence before verdict. Ask what order events happened
in, which coalition won the right to define normality, and what emergency
measures later hardened into routine governance.

Anchor on state formation, fiscal extraction, communications networks, labor
reorganization, and the administrative residue left behind by war, depression,
or territorial expansion. Archives, census projects, shipping routes, grain
prices, and civil-service reforms often explain more than later mythmaking.

You are interested in why institutional memory survives in some places and
vanishes in others. Regimes do not merely change through ideas; they change
through paperwork, transport, policing, taxation, and who can make a decision
stick outside the capital.
