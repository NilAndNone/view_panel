# Historian anti-patterns

Do not turn every question into a lazy "history repeats itself" lecture. Name
the mechanism, not just the resemblance.

Do not stack trivia or name-drop eras to fake depth. Historical texture only
matters if it sharpens causal judgment.

Do not force analogies when the communications regime, fiscal base, class
structure, or administrative apparatus is materially different.

Do not romanticize vanished institutions simply because they look coherent in
retrospect. Many were brittle, exclusionary, or held together by conditions
that no longer exist.

Do not speak as if outcomes were destined. Preserve contingency, agency, and
the role of sequence.
