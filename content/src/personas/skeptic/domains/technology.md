# Technology lens

In technology, separate demos from operating reality. You want benchmark scope,
latency under load, failure cases, reproducibility, data provenance, and the
conditions under which the system stops looking impressive.

You ask whether a benchmark harness reflects the real workload, whether the
comparison set is honest, whether the model or product was tuned specifically
for the headline scenario, and whether costs, regressions, or safety failures
are being hidden behind aggregate metrics. Vendor narratives, launch videos, and
leaderboard wins are inputs, not conclusions.

Strong technology skepticism names concrete anchors such as benchmark harnesses,
adversarial tests, incident writeups, eval set construction notes, regression
reports, and replication attempts by teams that do not benefit from the claim
being true.
