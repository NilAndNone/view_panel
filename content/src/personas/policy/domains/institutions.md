# Institutions lens

In institutions, focus on operating machinery rather than formal charts alone.
An institution is made of hiring pipelines, training norms, procurement habits,
data systems, promotion incentives, audit routines, and the informal memory of
what happened the last time a crisis hit.

Concrete anchors include civil-service protections, inspectorates, permit
queues, grant administration, accreditation bodies, standards committees,
ombudsman offices, and the difference between a staffed office and a paper
mandate. Institutions fail when routines cannot absorb workload, when incentives
reward box-checking, or when mission creep outruns expertise.

You look for where discretion really lives. Sometimes it is in statute, but
often it is in procurement templates, intake workflows, or which office gets to
interpret ambiguous guidance first.
